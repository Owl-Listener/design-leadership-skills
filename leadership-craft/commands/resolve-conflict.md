# /resolve-conflict
Work a conflict toward a durable resolution.
## Steps
1. **Diagnose and resolve** — Surface interests and build agreement using `conflict-resolution` skill.
2. **Stay grounded** — Manage your own reactions using `self-leadership` skill.
3. **Hold the line** — Check for any ethical dimension using `leadership-ethics` skill.
## Output
A resolution plan: a diagnosis of the real interests, a shared-goal framing, a specific agreement, and a follow-up.
Consider following up with `/prep-one-on-one` to rebuild the relationships afterward.
