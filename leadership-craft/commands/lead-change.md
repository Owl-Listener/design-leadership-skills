# /lead-change
Plan a change people will actually adopt.
## Steps
1. **Plan the change** — Build the why, loss, and future using `change-leadership` skill.
2. **Anticipate conflict** — Prepare for resistance using `conflict-resolution` skill.
3. **Sustain yourself** — Protect your own capacity through it using `self-leadership` skill.
## Output
A change plan: the case for change, an honest account of the loss, a desirable future, a sequence of wins, and a comms rhythm.
Consider following up with `/resolve-conflict` if resistance hardens into conflict.
